About Me

Hi, my name is Alexssandro, but you can call me Lele, whichever you prefer. I'm 16 years old and I'm from Brazil.

I'm bisexual and I prefer they/them pronouns, though I'm comfortable with any pronouns. My passion is drawing my OCs (Original Characters) and sharing them with the world.

I'm an LGBT ally and I love creating art that celebrates diversity and self-expression. Thank you for getting to know me! 💜
